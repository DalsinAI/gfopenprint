GFOpenPrint 0.4-rc2 (release candidate, 24 November 2026)

Prints from AmigaOS to the PDF printers on your network over IPP and IPPS,
and saves documents as PDF files. GFOpenView shows and prints pictures.

New in this candidate
- Since 0.4-rc1: OAPDiscover 0.4.1 and OAVWorker 0.3.1 say their new
  versions; BUILD-INFO.txt names the icon tools' commits too.
- Printers are found on GFOS again (BL-134). Printers and Queue found no
  printer on GFOS machines whose network is not in LAN address mode: the
  search sent its questions from the PC's own internal address, where no
  printer can answer. It now sends them from the LAN address. In a lab on
  the same LAN the old search had no replies; this one found both HP
  printers (12 replies).
- Since 0.3: pictures printed at the paper's resolution and Flate-compressed
  (#48), a stalled upload ends, GFOpenView's ARexx port with ACTIVATE,
  FAULT, VERSION and HELP (#47), ARexx return codes and no requesters
  (#49), GFOpenTLS by its GF name gfopentls.library (#45), Soft 3D package
  icons.

Built
- With GFOpenAmigaGCC 16.2.0-open9 (the kitchen's os32-gcc16-open9 stove)
  from gfopenprint main, by build-rc.sh. BUILD-INFO.txt in the package
  names the commit.

Not checked yet
- A printed page on paper from this candidate. Discovery and the PDF check
  were seen in a lab; physical printing is checked by hand.

Install: double-click "Install GFOpenPrint" in the drawer.
